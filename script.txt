SAT-SA PROTOTYPE DEMO - 3 MINUTE SCRIPT (v2)
=============================================
(About 440 words of narration at a calm pace. Record at 1080p, browser zoom 110%, bookmarks bar hidden.)

BEFORE RECORDING (once): reset the demo data so every detection appears:
  docker compose down -v && docker compose up -d --build     (wait ~1 minute, open http://localhost:8080)
  Local dev instead:  cd backend && python -m app.seed.generate_evidence --reset
Do the reset BEFORE recording - it wipes saved review actions.


[0:00 - 0:15]  OPENING - Overview page
ACTION: Slowly pan across the KPI cards.
SAY:
"This is SAT-SA, our Supervisory Analytics Tool for SOC Assessment. It runs fully
offline in two Docker containers, with no internet, no cloud and no external AI.
We've loaded nine months of SOC records from twenty critical-sector entities:
over eleven thousand alerts and ten thousand cases."


[0:15 - 0:35]  WHICH ENTITIES NEED ATTENTION
ACTION: Open "Entities", sort by risk.
SAY:
"The first question a supervisor asks is: where do I look first? Every entity gets
an explainable risk score built from execution gaps, negative space, peer deviation
and reporting integrity. Mature SOCs like HAL and BEL rank low; GAIL and the
under-reporting entities rise to the top."


[0:35 - 1:05]  BEHAVIOUR, NOT JUST RULES - Execution gaps page
ACTION: Open "Execution gaps". Point at the BPCL "EG-SLA-GAMING" row.
        Scroll down to the "Negative space" panel and point at the CPA "NS-OFFHOURS-BLIND" row.
SAY:
"SAT-SA detects behaviour that dashboards hide. BPCL closes sixty percent of its
critical cases in the thirty-six minutes just before the four-hour SLA - five times more
than just after it. The metric is driving the closure, not the investigation.
Below is negative space: evidence that should exist but doesn't. CPA has almost no
alerts at night while its peers detect around the clock - a monitoring blind spot."


[1:05 - 1:30]  PAPER VS PRACTICE
ACTION: Open "Paper vs practice". Point at the top rows and the "Overstated" card.
SAY:
"This is the problem statement's own definition of an execution gap: what an entity
reports versus what its evidence shows. NTPC declares ninety-five percent escalation
compliance; its own records show under half. Fourteen entities overstate their
controls. Honest, mature SOCs don't."


[1:30 - 2:05]  DRILL-DOWN AND EXAMINATION PLAN
ACTION: Click "bpcl". Scroll past compliance, funnel and peer radar to "Examination plan".
        Click a finding in "Findings with evidence lineage" to show source rows.
SAY:
"Drill into BPCL: compliance by control, the workflow funnel from alert to closure,
and the peer comparison. SAT-SA then writes the examination plan for the supervisor -
what to ask, which documents to request, and exactly which cases to pull first.
Every finding links back to the source records it was based on."


[2:05 - 2:20]  REVIEW, AUDIT, REPORT
ACTION: "Review queue" -> validate one finding -> "Audit trail" -> "Reports" -> download PDF.
SAY:
"Examiners validate or reject each finding. Every action is written to a hash-chained,
tamper-evident audit trail, and one click produces a reproducible PDF report."


[2:20 - 2:50]  VALIDATION - IS IT BETTER THAN MANUAL SAMPLING?
ACTION: Open "Validation". Point at the KPI cards, then trace the green curve vs the red line.
SAY:
"And we don't just claim it works - we measure it. Against ground truth, the risk
ranking separates weak from strong SOCs perfectly, and all eleven planted weaknesses
are detected. Compared with random manual sampling, a five percent sample chosen by
SAT-SA is four and a half times richer in deficient cases, and finding half of all
deficiencies takes twelve percent of the review effort instead of forty-eight."


[2:50 - 3:00]  CLOSING
ACTION: Return to "Overview".
SAY:
"SAT-SA doesn't replace the examiner. It tells them where to look, why, and with what
evidence - across hundreds of entities, fully offline."
